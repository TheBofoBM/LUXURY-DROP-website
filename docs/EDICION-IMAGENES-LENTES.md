## Prompt común aplicado a cada toma

```text
Use case: background-extraction.
Asset type: transparent e-commerce sunglasses product photograph.
Input image 1 is the EDIT TARGET, not just an inspiration. Edit this exact pair, preserving the exact original camera angle, frame and arms geometry, nosepiece, lens silhouette, visible side emblems and details, and the actual frame and mirrored lens colors. Do not redesign, add text, change brand, or invent parts.
Remove the entire room, couch, fabric, house and background. Output ONLY the complete sunglasses on true transparent alpha, including transparent openings between arms. Replace identifiable reflections of the house, outdoor scenery, TV, windows, camera and people inside the lenses with restrained neutral studio softbox highlights. Preserve the original lens tint, reflective metallic appearance and optical curvature. No identifiable scene reflected anywhere.
Clean realistic product photography, natural textures and soft studio illumination, sharp edges. Square canvas, sunglasses centered occupying about 82% of width with generous transparent margins, whole object visible without cropping. No backdrop, checkerboard, floor, props, cast floor shadow or watermark. Preserve this actual photographed angle; do not rotate the glasses to another view.
```
